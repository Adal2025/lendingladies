OUR STORY

LENDING LADIES

Lending Ladies was born over a glass of wine and a real conversation between two women navigating the highs and lows of corporate life. Co-founders Priscilla Picasso and Liz Castillo came together to create something they wished existed.

Whether you're climbing the corporate ladder, running your own business, or seeking clarity in your career journey, Lending Ladies is a place where you belong and where your voice matters.

To motivate, support, and uplift women through intentional networking events featuring inspiring speakers, meaningful dialogue, and opportunities for real connection.
